# Column detail study — public-source comparison

Scope: column only. A gate made from separate columns and a lintel remains an untested future hypothesis. No gate or topology/window experiment is executed here.

## What the primary sources establish

- [IAAC Mesh Subdivision](https://www.iaacblog.com/programs/mesh-subdivision/) explicitly combines subdivision and a Mandelbrot definition. Its catalogue varies the number/powers of field volumes, initial geometry, depth, subdivision and added attractor curves. The public diagrams support repeatedly subdividing and moving coordinates relative to the field. They do not publish sufficient executable detail to recover the exact 3D formula or subdivision weights. Its +1/+2 catalogues are parameter/input variations, not a controlled fixed-input chronology.
- [Hansmeyer, Subdivided Columns](https://www.michael-hansmeyer.com/subdivided-columns) describes articulation across overall form, local formations and microstructure, with tagged input components receiving different parameters. Its published 9x detail image shows geometrically faceted surfaces with larger curving formations and smaller nested cells together. The useful lesson is differentiated scale and location, rather than a target face count. The page does not give a reproducible coefficient schedule.
- [Hansmeyer, Digital Grotesque II](https://www.michael-hansmeyer.com/digital-grotesque-II) and [ETH DBT](https://center.dbt.arch.ethz.ch/digital-grotesque-ii/) describe topological subdivision: porous, layered structures and connections between voids. That is an additional capability beyond refining and displacing a closed genus-zero surface. We do not claim to reproduce it. Existing Tissue/frame assets were audited and left separate after the user's clarification; see `topology_asset_notes.md`.

## Direct visual cross-check

The original images inspected were the linked column 9x detail, a Digital Grotesque II printed detail, and IAAC's Mandelbrot/attractor/+1 board. Reference images remain in the local temporary source-view folder, not in the repository's result boards. All result images are renders of CHESHIRE's explicit saved triangles.

The column 9x image matters because it also contains dense faceting. Dense faceting is therefore not an automatic failure criterion. The question is whether it belongs to larger coherent folds and differentiated formations. Digital Grotesque II's visible perforations must not be mistaken for our surface cavities. IAAC already has fine crust in some +1 cases; a universal claim that its detail always appears later would be unsupported.

## CHESHIRE implementations and test differences

- CONTINUED: actual OVERLAP G3 controls → one existing Task36 split → one existing finite nearest-outside field pass. The field's polar power-4 classifier is our public-flow reconstruction, not IAAC's recovered equation. Internal escape count remains 18. G4 field budget is .16; local outside-target refinement changes from source G3's factor 3 to 2. This is a declared recurrence continuation, not a one-variable causal ablation of subdivision alone.
- NESTED: same actual G3 controls → existing bilinear split and E/F normal-role placement, restricted to selected guide-adjacent regions. Old controls are held; no further field pass. Local support scales the new relief, with narrow guide-core protection and no global activation floor. Global mesh generation and local role-pass count are recorded separately. It tests selective fine geometry, not continued Mandelbrot recursion.

Neither path implements a physical origami solver, permanent crease-angle memory, automatic invention of new macro folds, or fabrication validation. Holding old control coordinates does not prove preservation of every point on the previous rendered fan surface. No separate geometric smoothing, random-noise texture, image-generated detail, or normal map is used.

## Completed comparisons

- NESTED_STRONG uses exactly the same source, regions and split as NESTED, with existing E/F coefficients multiplied by three (.66 / -1.56). Actual maximum displacement is .084852 model units rather than .028284. It makes real local relief, but the resulting cells read as stars or thorns rather than continuous returning folds. Both branches stop at G4.
- RETURN_DETAIL uses one existing bilinear split and two small existing radial/axial PCHIP return events beside the inherited large folds. It is an authored geometric comparison, not a new fractal-emergent folding law. The original event's fixed angular offset and terminal envelope remain. Unlike NESTED, selected old controls can move.
- RETURN_HYBRID uses the exact saved post-return G4 controls and adds one existing finite-18 field pass, without another split or repeated return. This isolates the last field operation within the pair. Only 658 of the 4,868 return-active controls move under that field. Survival of the small returns therefore does not establish general crease memory. Several existing central chevrons weaken, so this is not an overall aesthetic improvement.

All five new candidates stop at subdivision generation four. Their field-pass totals differ: CONTINUED and RETURN_HYBRID have four; the other three have three. The classifier's internal escape count of 18 is neither 18 generations nor 18 physical folds. No G5, gate, Tissue or window variant was generated.

## Preservation and sampling limits

The inherited guide mask attenuates new returns by up to approximately 95%, but no control reaches its exact-lock predicate. The recorded zero moved fully-protected controls is vacuous because the fully-protected count is also zero. Of 1,780 controls within .018 model units of the actual guide curves, 600 move during RETURN_DETAIL, by at most .04136. The subsequent field has no corresponding feature-lock mask. This is partial geometric protection, not exact legacy-crease preservation.

An array-only sampling diagnostic measured bilinear child mean-fan centres against the previous four-triangle quad fans, before new relief. Distances have median .0004323, p99 .0030849 and maximum .0098020 model units; 20.21% exceed .001. This is comparable to weak NESTED's .0007158 median nonzero placement. It shows why fixed old control coordinates alone do not guarantee unchanged rendered surfaces. It is not an XYZ smoothing pass or a full surface Hausdorff measurement. The existing native VF fan option was identified but not tested here; no solution is claimed.

Core checks on RETURN_DETAIL and RETURN_HYBRID confirm X/Y reflected vertex pairing and identical reflected triangle sets, one connected closed surface with no nonmanifold edges, and self-intersection witnesses in both. The checker stops after detecting witnesses; its returned pair-list length is not an intersection count. The weak branches retain intermediate checks only. Source G3 already has confirmed self-intersections. Thickness, structure and fabrication remain unverified.
