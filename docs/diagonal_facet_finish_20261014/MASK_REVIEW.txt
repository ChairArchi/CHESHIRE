READ-ONLY VISUAL COVERAGE AUDIT / 2026-10-14

The initial source-based selective shading mask does not adequately preserve
the user's preferred broad comb pattern. It smooths much of that pattern even
before the candidate's native centers are modified. Therefore its render pair
cannot establish improvement over the user's original all-FLAT reference.

Measured approximate-visible source-quad ROI in actual 900-square macro:
lost lower-right strip [475,700,740,899]: 1,356 quads; 134 flat (9.88%).
Long-axis abs(Z)<=.15: 598; >=.95: 2.
Median aspect 1.676; corresponding median aspect gain about .0135.
580 quads have aspect<=1.5; aspect gain<.35 excludes 805 faces.
Diagonal gain<.35 affects 717; warpage gain<.35 affects 250.
Only 9 faces fail geometric safety. These counts overlap; do not add them.

Surviving right rim [775,700,885,875]: 1,322 quads; 295 flat (22.31%).
Median aspect 3.125 and long-axis abs(Z)=.718 are favored by the mask.

Conclusion: world-axis direction and source-quad anisotropy are weak proxies
for the visible native triangle comb. Nearly isotropic quads and quads whose
long axis is near horizontal can carry the preferred rendered diagonal pattern.
The mask remains frozen as a documented failed coverage selection. No threshold
sweep or candidate regeneration was performed in this audit.

Same-candidate all-FLAT renders are required to assess the actual geometric
accent while preserving the original comb appearance. This adds a presentation
comparison only, not another geometry candidate or Mandelbulb experiment.
