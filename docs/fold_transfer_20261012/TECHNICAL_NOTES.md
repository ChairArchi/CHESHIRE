## Interpretation and checks

Actual whole and meso renders favor RETURN_ONLY for clear shoulders and valleys. Protection retains central layers but introduces denser cell zigzags. A plausible mechanism is differential movement between pinned inherited points and active newborn points; this is an interpretation, not a separately isolated causal experiment. Small fixed front/side zone crops show little field response and do not demonstrate global crease preservation. Surface retention, numerical point protection and aesthetic success are distinct.

Two selected RETURN_ONLY meshes received core checks on authoritative float64 `geometry.npz`: one closed connected surface, consistent winding, no boundary/nonmanifold edges, exact reflected triangle sets X/Y, error<4.25e-15, minimum area>1e-12. CGAL detects self-intersection witnesses in both. `first_only` returns witnesses, not exhaustive totals. Other G4 variants have intermediate integrity/render checks only. Fabrication, thickness and structural performance remain unverified. This G4 assessment initially stopped further generations; the subsequent user-directed bounded G5 test is recorded separately in G5_REPORT.md and G5_INDEX.json.

The small `--input` adapter accepts another G0 on the documented chart/topology/lineage. It does not remesh arbitrary inputs and has not been exercised on a third input. Height10 reference units, field placement, two-event schedule and the existing return kernel bound the demonstrated scope. The G5 schedule was a proposal at commit1d8673f; it was subsequently executed unchanged in the dedicated follow-up.

IAAC's public subdivision/field loop remains the reference, not recovered executable code. Its exact equation/weights are not claimed here; see the preserved [primary-source comparison](../column_detail_20261012/SOURCE_NOTES.md). Current result images contain only CHESHIRE's saved triangle geometry with uniform material and flat geometric normals. Identical whole cameras and fixed close-up coordinates are recorded by the existing render scripts.
