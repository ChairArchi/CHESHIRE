# Independent visual review

Reviewed actual front, oblique and side renders of SPAN_RETURN_FINE, BAND_COUPLED and BAND_REGIONAL, G1?G3. Camera conditions match. This is a visual assessment only, not an intersection or fabrication check.

- **BAND_COUPLED:** strongest of these new comparisons. It removes most early shallow granular crust and exposes the concave inner channels, returned rims and continuous broad surfaces. Deep folds are still readable in oblique and side views.
- **What it does not establish:** G1?G3 changes of silhouette and meso structure remain modest. The clearest later change is the upper shoulder widening; this does not demonstrate emergence of a new nested hierarchy. This result primarily organizes new displacement rather than solves recursive architectural growth.
- **BAND_REGIONAL:** quieter and more conservative. It suppresses crust, but its visual gain over COUPLED is small and the upper body has longer smooth stretches. It is useful as an activation control, not a clearly stronger sculpture.
- **Common remaining limits:** repeated cups stay legible as separate events; narrow central neck and its transverse lip do not strongly interlock the upper/lower systems. The side retains broad faceted channels rather than the more continuous front logic. Base/capital still read as thick cuffs with rough terminal rims.
- **Loss relative to baseline:** less local material incident and irregular density. This loss is favorable for legibility now, but the replacement is not yet a convincing second scale of detail.
- **Recommendation:** preserve COUPLED as a cleaner starting condition for a separately motivated role-dependent subdivision trial. Do not increase these band stages merely to report generation count. Keep baseline and REGIONAL as controls.

No image-based claim about self-intersection, watertightness, thickness, or physical folding is made.
