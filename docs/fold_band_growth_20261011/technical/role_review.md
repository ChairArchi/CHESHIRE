# Role-dependent subdivision: independent visual assessment

Actual G00 source and G1?G3 front, oblique, side renders reviewed for ROLE_VERTEX_FACE and ROLE_EDGE_FACE.

Both trials make a clear geometry change but chiefly at the regular cell scale. VERTEX_FACE reads as horizontal terraces and rectangular relief; EDGE_FACE reads as closely packed diamonds. The initial deep valleys survive, but the principal cup, waist and shoulder relations barely develop. Later generations mainly subdivide the visual grid. This is not evidence of an integrated new meso hierarchy.

Increasing gain at this same sampling density is unlikely to solve the scale problem: it would enlarge the cell relief rather than change its organizational logic. If one additional bounded trial is made, a one-level coarser carrier with the same support-relative coefficient is more informative. Inspect G1 before continuing. Stop if it remains a tiled shell; also reject any apparent success purchased by losing the source deep grooves through undersampling.

No physical-folding or self-intersection conclusion is derived from the renders.
