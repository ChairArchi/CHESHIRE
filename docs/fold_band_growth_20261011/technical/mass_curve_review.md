# MASS_CURVE G1: independent visual review

Actual front, oblique and side renders reviewed.

The large mass genuinely changes: an enlarged upper body dominates a tightened central neck and a smaller lower expansion. This is macro movement, rather than merely surface relief.

The visual cost outweighs that change. The separation between an outward lip, an inward valley, and a returned surface weakens. Thick inflated bodies dominate the front and oblique views. Some deep side fissures remain, but they do not restore the three-dimensional folding logic across views.

Record this as an unsuccessful sculptural direction with a real mass-displacement result. Stopping before G2 is justified. No expensive core validation is needed to decide whether to pursue this direction; no image-based topology/intersection claim is made.
