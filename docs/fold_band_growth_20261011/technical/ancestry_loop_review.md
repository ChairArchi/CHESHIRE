# Existing Task36 ancestry-aware coupled placement + field: bounded reuse test

Generated and inspected actual front, oblique and side renders at G1, G2 and G3 from the same SPAN_SPARSE quad input. Source operators were not changed. Each cycle uses exactly one Task36 coupled quad split followed by the same finite n4 nearest-outside field as FULL_LOOP: 18 internal escape iterations, fraction .55, budgets .35/.28/.22, locally refined targets 1/2/3.

Task36 standard CC averaging component is disabled (gain 0). Existing weighted stencils and support-scaled normal placement remain; these do combine coordinates, so this is not a claim that no averaging occurs anywhere. Old vertices are held exactly during Task36 by vertex_memory=1, then remain eligible for field movement. Classes, rest and original-face anchors are preserved in control_quad.npz, while immediate parent-face indices are saved in operation_fields.npz. The existing inherited V/E/F diagonal pattern is eligible for 0/400 faces at G1, 1600/1600 at G2 and 6400/6400 at G3. This is subdivision ancestry, not semantic crease-memory or a physical fold solver.

The prescribed single recipe was used unchanged: face_gain .65, edge_gain .18, feedback 1, inherited_bias .8, diagonal_tension -.65, edge_tension .5, vertex_tension -.8, ancestry_gain 1, stencil_gain 1 and normal_gain 1. The original plane_only terminal constraint remains during Task36; cap face displacement and terminal normal controls are suppressed, terminal V/E Z components constrained. The subsequent field is free to move those points. It is therefore not a free-cap equivalent of FULL_LOOP.

Visually, G2 develops stronger horizontal lips and rectangular channels across the shoulder, waist and side recess than the simpler birth-role FULL_LOOP. These cross the original vertical paths, making the side organization more consistent. G3 retains those larger ledges and introduces finer subdivisions along them; it does not collapse into puffy smooth mass or completely random crust. This is a useful difference worth preserving.

Limits remain clear: two dominant bodies, cup relationships and the main silhouette change little. Regular belts and grid repetition can dominate; broad body facets persist. The upper and lower mouths become very dark, while neck and terminals accumulate a cage-like sequence. G3 is a meaningful comparison, but not proof of IAAC-level macro/meso emergence or exact reproduction. No further generations were run.

Quick geometry checks: all stages finite, one connected component, watertight and winding-consistent; reflected triangle sets match X/Y. G3 has 51,202 vertices and 102,400 triangles, minimum area 1.68845e-6 against unchanged threshold 1e-12, reflection error below 3.75e-15. Task36 maximum new-placement steps are .32665/.27071/.20114. Self-intersection was NOT checked in this reuse probe; thickness, structure and fabrication remain NOT checked.
