# Field-seed + curve-directed role growth: independent review

Actual FIELD_SEED G01 and CURVE_CIRCLE/CURVE_SWEEP G1?G2 front, oblique and side renders were reviewed. These two curve trials share the starting mesh, role recipe and stage count, so their difference supports a comparison of attractor distribution. Comparisons to ROLE_ABSOLUTE are design comparisons, not a controlled attribution: input, subdivision density, and activation differ.

**CIRCLE G2:** three height bands concentrate deep cellular lips and leave quieter large folded surfaces between them. It gives the clearest large-surface/local-network contrast. The cost is that some bands read as separate decorative collars.

**SWEEP G2:** the cellular network extends along more of the height and develops around front, side, and terminal regions. Its spatial continuity is more assertive, but denser walls increasingly precede the original folds in visual attention. The overall relief distribution is less differentiated than CIRCLE.

Both establish deeper hierarchy on an existing folded surface, not a reorganization of the column's large mass or a new node relationship. The sweeping curves change where relief activates; the renders do not establish that the curves generate new large folding paths.

Preserve CIRCLE G2 as the contrast/hierarchy representative and SWEEP G2 as the more connected intense-relief alternative. Neither should be presented as the same implementation as the IAAC source. No image-based intersection or fabrication conclusion is made.
