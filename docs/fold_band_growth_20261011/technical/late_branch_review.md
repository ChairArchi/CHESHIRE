# Late cross-branch review: independent visual selection

Viewed the saved IAAC main7 and main9 source boards first, then actual front/oblique/side renders of DUAL_FIELD_LOOP and FIELD_STAGED G2/G3, ANCESTRY_LOOP G2/G3 and FULL_LOOP G2/G3. No other agent review was read before this assessment. This memo concerns visual form only; it makes no connectivity, collision, thickness or structural claim from images.

## At most two leads

1. **ANCESTRY_LOOP G3**: best balance of continuous large folds, legible cross-ledges and a deeper secondary network. Compared with G2, the stronger vertical and horizontal lines subdivide the waist and side recess while the larger cups and dark mouths remain readable. Broad body facets remain, but they do not visibly spread into a newly flattened overall mass. G3 adds some regular cage/belt repetition; preserve G2 as the quieter version. This is the most persuasive continuation of the current fold language, although its main two-body silhouette barely develops.

2. **DUAL_FIELD_LOOP G2**: strongest distinct geometric alternative. Deep rectangular/diagonal cell walls articulate front, shoulder and side, and the large-cell boundaries are clear. The side recess retains a coherent continuous depth rather than becoming an unrelated rough back. Its weakness is large shield-like central panels and a rather uniform frame-grid language. G3 adds many smaller cells and fractured rims, but the stronger crust around waist, crown and base weakens the clean G2 scale contrast. The deep recess remains visible; it is not simply filled solid in the image. Central planar panels persist and become strongly framed, so G3 does not resolve the flat-body risk. Keep G3 as a limit comparison, not the default next lead.

## Other branches

**FIELD_STAGED G2 to G3**: shallow centre chevrons and small creases become more visible, while the overall mass and large quiet bands remain almost identical. The front cup mouths read shallower and less open than ANCESTRY/DUAL, although the side still shows a deep longitudinal recess. The broad front/oblique surfaces and unchanged collar bands make this a weak candidate for current architectural articulation. Less noise is useful, but it has not produced a correspondingly stronger large fold. Do not extend it merely because it is cleaner.

**FULL_LOOP G2 to G3**: additional rumpling accumulates on shoulders, rims and side recess, with large smooth collar/waist bands still interrupting the hierarchy. G2 remains the clearer reference. G3 shows a working repeated operation but offers less legible meso development than ANCESTRY or DUAL G2.

## Reading the IAAC comparison responsibly

The source boards show several different balances: main7 includes very dense crust, while main9 includes both broad fold-preserving columns and highly cellular surfaces. Main9's right-side examples retain elongated pockets and alternating large/small regions rather than presenting every surface at one scale. These are useful visual targets. However, the columns have differing settings and inputs, so main7 to main9 is not a controlled same-input G1-to-G2 progression. No original IAAC recipe or causal mechanism is inferred from their appearance here.

## Next design implication

Preserve the strong large recesses and broad ledges of ANCESTRY while studying the deeper, cleaner cell contrast of DUAL G2. Avoid another unchanged loop whose main gain is the number of small cells. A worthwhile next change would visibly redirect or differentiate a whole shoulder/waist/cup relation; uniform fine crust or merely cleaner broad planes would not qualify. This is an evaluation criterion, not a new algorithm prescription.
