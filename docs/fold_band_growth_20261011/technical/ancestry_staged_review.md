# ANCESTRY_STAGED: one bounded integration comparison

## Actual implementation and isolation

Ran the root-owned ancestry_probe.py unchanged with --name ANCESTRY_STAGED --staged-field, first G1/G2, inspected all actual views, then continued G3 because the deep side recesses were still legible. --late-return was NOT used. Maximum authored-return displacement is exactly zero at every stage.

The comparison is ANCESTRY_LOOP (internal field iterations 18,18,18) versus ANCESTRY_STAGED (4,8,18). Same original SPAN_SPARSE input, existing Task36 coupled recipe, one quad split per cycle, ancestry anchors, fraction .55, budgets .35/.28/.22, local target factors 1/2/3, terminal plane_only policy and output cameras. No source operator was edited. There are THREE growth cycles in both cases; the internal escape counts are not numbers of folding generations.

Fresh matching field banks are selected per staged cycle: F02(iter4), F03(iter8), F01(iter18). Lower finite iterations expand occupancy and alter target geometry. This is a declared CHESHIRE hypothesis, NOT a pure frequency filter and NOT an IAAC disclosed schedule.

Task36 ancestry first becomes eligible at G2 (1600/1600 faces), then G3 (6400/6400). G1 has no eligible ancestry faces. Original 400 cage-face anchor lineage is retained. This is V/E/F ancestry-weighted placement, not crease-feature memory. Existing Task36 old vertices remain fixed during its placement, but later field passes are free to move them. Standard averaging gain remains zero; no extra XYZ smoothing or endpoint modification was added.

## Actual front / oblique / side reading

At G2 staged relief broadens over the upper and lower bodies. The small dark front punctures in fixed LOOP become shallower-looking nested chevrons and paired lower windows. The deep side cup/recess division survives; there is no global collapse into a smooth cushion. Front broad folding is somewhat more legible, while side and waist retain the obvious rectilinear cell organization of the ancestry operator.

At G3 this difference remains despite the same final 18-iteration classifier. The staged upper front has broader nested relief, and the lower folded cup wraps around separated openings. It does not simply make every groove deeper: the original dark front punctures are lost. G2 to G3 chiefly develops smaller cell relief. The two-body/cup/waist macro rhythm is essentially retained; no new macro node appears. Repeated grid articulation remains a design limitation.

Partial value: combining field staging with an actual ancestry-aware operator gives a calmer large-relief distribution while preserving side depth. It is not full IAAC reproduction or proof of crease memory. No further branches were generated.
