# Three paired growth cycles: independent visual review

Reviewed FIELD_SEED and FULL_LOOP actual front, oblique and side renders at G1, G2 and G3. Each FULL_LOOP generation combines one quad refinement, one role-dependent placement and one fractal-field movement; this is three paired growth cycles, not six generations. FIELD_SEED is the same field/refinement control without role placement.

The added role operation leaves visible transverse lips and cellular channels at the upper shoulder, central waist and lower rim. In the side view, the central recess is more organized than the field-only control. These are real secondary surface differences.

The large silhouette, two dominant bodies, waist and cup relationships remain nearly the same. The later changes primarily add irregular shallow terrain and subdivisions of existing cellular walls, while broad planar facets persist. The comparison therefore does not establish new macro organization. FULL_LOOP G2 is the more legible paired-loop representative; G3 records the limit of accumulating roughness.

The earlier sequential CIRCLE G2 has stronger deep-cell contrast, but its role depth and timing differ. It is a design comparison only: these images cannot isolate whether repeated field application suppresses role offsets. Nor does this recipe establish a mathematical incompatibility between the field and subdivision.

Conclusion: a coupled three-cycle mechanism was implemented and does visibly differ from field-only growth, but this bounded result does not yet achieve the convincing hierarchy sought from the IAAC reference. Preserve the actual controls and generations without presenting texture accumulation as large-scale formal development.

No topology, intersection, thickness or fabrication conclusion is inferred from these images.
