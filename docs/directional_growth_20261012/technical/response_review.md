OVERLAP G3 with class_signed remains the visual lead. Compared under the same three cameras, FACE_RESPONSE and SIGNED_RESPONSE soften the nested frontal lips, especially within the upper central panel. Their broader convex panels read more like an inflated body carrying folds at its edges. The original retains a clearer sequence of inward/outward steps, so its repeated cells participate more strongly in the large fold rather than merely surrounding it.

Both response variants retain the long side recess and the lower shoulder intervention. They therefore do not collapse into an entirely flat body, and their quieter central panels may be useful as a secondary material contrast. However, neither adds a convincing new silhouette event or improves the transition from the lower blades through the waist to the upper mass. At this review scale FACE_RESPONSE and SIGNED_RESPONSE are close; I see no strong visual reason to prefer one or to advance either beyond the existing G3.

Preserve them as a bounded comparison of existing response modes, with OVERLAP G2/G3 retained as the stronger design pair. No G4 is justified by this comparison. This is a visual assessment of the supplied mesh renders; it does not establish self-intersection, fabrication, or structural performance for the two new response variants.
