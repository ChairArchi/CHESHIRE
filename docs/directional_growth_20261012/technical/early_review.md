# Directional growth G2: independent early review

Viewed early_comparison.png and the actual 800-pixel front, oblique and side renders of CORRIDOR/G02 and OVERLAP/G02 against the previous ANCESTRY_STAGED/G02. **OVERLAP warrants one G3 test.** The upper recess extends toward the waist, while the lower body's shoulders turn outward and introduce a different directional event within the existing fold language. The side keeps its long deep recess and adds a sharper lower wedge/return; it does not visually collapse into an unrelated rough side. The new shoulder does not read as a separate circumferential cuff like the previous late-waist-return trial.

OVERLAP is not an unqualified improvement: the paired lower blades are strong enough to become ornamental wings, and the large shield-like front planes remain. It has changed meso-scale hierarchy and silhouette locally, rather than producing a whole new macro organization. The worthwhile G3 question is whether subsequent field/subdivision can integrate and articulate this already changed mass without filling the deep side groove. Repeating or amplifying the same large authored return risks turning it into a stacked collar or palm-like motif. Preserve the G2 even if G3 loses clarity.

CORRIDOR reduces fine lattice and makes some large folds easier to isolate, but the broad front and oblique planes now read more exposed and block-like. The side recess is still deep, yet its secondary wall relations are weaker. Its quieter surface is therefore not sufficient evidence of a better hierarchy; another unchanged generation has low priority. These are visual judgments only. No topology, self-intersection or structural conclusions are inferred from the renders.
