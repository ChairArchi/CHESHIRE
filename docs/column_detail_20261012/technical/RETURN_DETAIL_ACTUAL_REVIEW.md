# Small authored-return comparison

Reviewed actual front, oblique, side and fixed front z6.2 / side z3.5 close-ups for both G4 candidates.

RETURN_DETAIL adds a small opposed lip beside the upper front groove and a returning rim/pocket beside the lower side shoulder. These are distinct from NESTED's triangle-cell spikes. The whole silhouette is almost unchanged (width about +0.3%, depth about +1.5% versus G3). The local edges also look thin and somewhat torn; broad panels and the existing grid remain. This is limited local progress, not a demonstrated new automatic growth hierarchy.

RETURN_HYBRID starts from the exact saved post-return G4 controls and adds only the existing finite-18 field (.55 fraction, .16 budget, target factor2), without another split or duplicated event. Its two small returns remain readable, but the field moves only 658 of the 4,868 return-active controls (13.5%). This is one-pass survival under partial field exposure, not general crease memory. Field/subdivision totals are 4/4 for HYBRID and 3/4 for DETAIL.

The inherited guide mask attenuates the new events by up to about 95%; it does not fully lock any control in this run. The exact-lock count is zero. Within .018 of the actual G3 guide curves, 600 of 1,780 controls move during the authored return, at most .04136. The later field can move these points independently. No threshold or geometry was changed after inspection.

Both G4 meshes have 204,802 vertices / 409,600 triangles, one connected closed surface, consistent winding, finite coordinates and X/Y reflection error below 5.36e-15 in generation checks. Minimum triangle areas are 1.65e-7 (DETAIL) and 3.34e-8 (HYBRID), against the unchanged 1e-12 threshold. Subsequent core_validation.json checks confirm self-intersection in both meshes. CGAL detect_only first_only reports positive witnesses rather than an exhaustive intersection count. Thickness, structural performance and fabrication feasibility remain unverified.

Stop after this pair; no G5. Preserve OVERLAP G3 as the large-form reference and these G4 results as selective authored-return assets. The returns are composed geometric displacement using the existing PCHIP radial/axial sequence, not fractal-emergent folding or an origami solver. No original operator or renderer was edited.
